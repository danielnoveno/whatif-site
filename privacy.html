Privacy Policy — WhatIf Auto Upload

  1. What is collected: the app receives your TikTok user identifier, nickname and avatar through the Login Kit scope user.info.basic, plus an OAuth access/refresh token stored locally on the owner's device.
  2. What is NOT collected: no email, phone, password, contacts, direct messages, or viewing activity. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.
  3. Data use: tokens are used solely to publish video files created by the owner to the owner's own account.
  4. Retention: tokens remain until revoked by the owner in TikTok Settings → Privacy → Authorized apps, or deleted from the local machine.
  5. Contact: the app owner via their TikTok account.
